For music nonprofits

A network of music nonprofits

GMI connects music nonprofits around the world through a shared library of expert teaching. Each partner keeps its own name, mission and programs, and becomes an ambassador for a larger cause.

Ask about joiningSee the vision
The larger vision

One library of teaching, shared across organizations

Small music nonprofits do extraordinary work, but most can't afford a library of professional teaching or the technology to deliver it.

GMI builds that once and shares it with every partner. Masterclasses from professional musicians and Hulili, GMI's AI music coach, become available to students across the whole network, whether they're in Miami, rural Tennessee or Nepal.

Partners also share something just as valuable, their own music and traditions. Over time, the library grows with teaching from every community in the network, so students learn from each other's cultures, not just from one place.

What it means to be a partner

Partners get

Shared masterclasses and Hulili

Access for the partner's students to GMI's masterclass library and Hulili, the AI coach, as each becomes available.

In development
Partners get

A place in a larger cause

A connection to music nonprofits in other places working toward the same goal, and a shared story that helps small organizations stand out to students and funders.

Partners share

Teaching and traditions

Partners can help grow the library by connecting GMI with teaching artists and sharing their own music, always with the artist's and community's permission.

Partners stay fully independent. Joining GMI doesn't change who runs your organization, how you raise money or how you serve your students.

Our first partner

The Initiatives Foundation in Nepal

GMI's first partnership is with The Initiatives Foundation, led by Director Karun Dhakal.

Together, we're documenting the Gandharva sarangi storytelling tradition with the musicians who carry it. Fieldwork is planned for late April to mid-June 2027.
