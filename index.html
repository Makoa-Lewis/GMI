What we do

Music is how people find each other.

Connect

Preserve

Open doors

We work to expand access to music education for students in underserved and rural communities.

Flagship project · Nepal

The Sarangi Documentation Project

In a rural, high-altitude region of Nepal, Gandharva musicians carry an oral storytelling tradition on the sarangi, a bowed string instrument. Much of it has never been fully recorded.

With The Initiatives Foundation, we're creating the first thorough documentary and written record of this tradition.

About us

Started at Vanderbilt, built for the world.

GMI began through Immersion Vanderbilt with one goal: use music to connect people and create real change.

Our founder, Makoa Lewis, grew up in rural Hockinson, Washington, and found his path through percussion. At 15 he was accepted to the YOLA National Festival and performed alongside the LA Phil and Gustavo Dudamel. GMI exists to open that same kind of door for others.
