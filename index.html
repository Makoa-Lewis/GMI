Why GMI exists

Talent is everywhere. Teachers aren't.

In many small towns, a school has one music director and no private teachers. Lessons are far away, expensive, or both.

GMI's founder grew up in exactly that kind of place. He didn't take a private lesson until college. GMI exists so the next student doesn't have to wait that long.

Founder

Makoa Lewis

A percussionist from rural Hockinson, Washington, who found his way to the world stage, and is building the doors he wishes he'd had.

At 15, Makoa was accepted to the YOLA National Festival and performed alongside the LA Phil and Gustavo Dudamel. Today he's a QuestBridge Scholar at Vanderbilt University studying Human and Organizational Development and Culture, Advocacy, and Leadership, with a minor in Percussion Performance.
