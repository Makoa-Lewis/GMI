His story

From a small town to the world stage.

Makoa Lewis is a percussionist, a student at Vanderbilt University, and the founder of the Global Music Initiative.

He grew up in Hockinson, a rural community in southwest Washington, where he started playing percussion. At 15 he was accepted to the YOLA National Festival, where he performed alongside the LA Phil and Gustavo Dudamel.

That experience showed him what can happen when a young musician gets a real opportunity.

At Vanderbilt, he developed GMI through Immersion Vanderbilt to create more of those opportunities: connecting communities through music and expanding access to music education for students in underserved and rural areas. He is leading GMI's first project, the Sarangi Documentation Project in Nepal.
