For partners, sponsors and investors

Help bring expert music lessons to students who've never had them.

GMI is early, and we're honest about that. Here's the problem, how GMI works, where we stand, and where your support makes the biggest difference.

Start a conversation
The problem

Talent is everywhere. Access isn't.

In many rural and low-income communities, a school has one music director and no private teachers nearby.

Private lessons are where most serious young musicians grow, and they're often out of reach because of cost, distance or both. Students with real talent stall out, not because they can't do it, but because no one is there to show them the next step.

The model

Expert teaching that can reach any student, at a low cost per student.

Professional teaching is recorded once and reaches every student. Hulili helps each student use it. Volunteer mentors provide the human connection.

Masterclasses

Professional teaching, shared widely

Recorded lessons from professional musicians, used with signed artist agreements and credit.

Hulili

An AI coach, always available

Answers students' questions from GMI's masterclass library and suggests what to practice next.

Mentors

Volunteers from university chapters

Trained, background-checked students who guide each GMI student toward instruments, teachers and opportunities.

Because mentors are volunteers, GMI's main costs are technology, masterclass content, mentor screening and a small fund for students' instruments and opportunities. See how it works in detail
