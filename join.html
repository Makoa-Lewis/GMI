Financial support

Cost shouldn't decide who gets to learn.

Financial support is available for students who can demonstrate financial need.

If you're requesting support, the application asks a few short questions about your situation. We don't ask for tax forms or bank statements online.

If we need more information, a GMI staff member will follow up privately with the student and their parent or guardian. Everything you share is kept confidential and used only to decide financial support.

Students under 18 need a parent or guardian to complete this application with them.
