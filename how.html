How it works

Lessons from professionals. A coach that's always there. A real person in your corner.

GMI combines three things most students in small towns never get together. Here's how they fit, and where each one stands today.

A student's path

From application to the next open door.

  1. Apply

    A student applies with a parent or guardian. Financial support is available for students who can demonstrate financial need.

  2. Learn

    Students watch masterclasses from professional musicians, at their own pace.

  3. Practice with Hulili

    Hulili answers questions, explains technique and suggests what to practice next.

  4. Meet a mentor

    A trained GMI mentor checks in about twice a month and helps the student set goals.

  5. Open doors

    Mentors help students find instruments, teachers, programs and college advice.

Part 1

Masterclasses

Seeking first artists

The heart of GMI is teaching from people who do this for a living.

Masterclasses are lessons recorded by professional musicians on technique, practice habits, performance and musicianship. Students can watch them anytime, as many times as they need.

  • Every masterclass is used only with a signed agreement from the artist
  • Artists are credited whenever Hulili draws on their teaching
  • GMI is now reaching out to its first artists

Part 2

Hulili, the AI coach

In development

Hulili is a Hawaiian word for a bridge or ladder across a gully. It's a way across a gap, which is what GMI hopes to be for students.

Hulili lets students ask questions in their own words, like "How do I get faster without getting tense?", and get a clear answer drawn from GMI's masterclass library, with a link to the lesson it came from.

  • Answers come from GMI's own library of permitted masterclasses
  • Hulili points students to real people whenever a question needs one
  • GMI staff can review conversations to keep answers safe and accurate

Hulili isn't live yet. The previews on this site use sample answers so you can see how it will work. Try the preview

Part 3

Real mentors

Recruiting now

Mentors aren't music teachers. They're guides who help each student find what they need next.

GMI mentors are student volunteers from GMI's university chapters, starting at Vanderbilt. A mentor might help a student find an instrument loan program, a local teacher, a summer camp or a scholarship, or simply help them stay motivated.

  • Every mentor completes a background check and training first
  • About two online check-ins a month, for at least one semester
  • Each student has a backup mentor, so no one is left without support

Safety

Built for young students from day one.

  • Students under 18 join with a parent or guardian's permission
  • Mentoring happens online in GMI's portal, never in private one-on-one channels
  • Student information stays private and is never sold or shared
  • GMI staff can see mentor check-ins and step in whenever needed
